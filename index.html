Shujia Liang (Nathan)

Core Contributor, OpenRSI Foundation

I am a core contributor at OpenRSI Foundation, a non-profit aiming towards open-source infrastructure of recursive self-improvement. My interests span AI, computational physics, statistics, chemistry, history, blockchain, and political economy.

I am currently contributing to Agent's Last Exam V2 (Benchmark), OpenRSI-Index (Artifacts), and OpenRSI-X-Index (Coming soon). If you are interested in contributing any scientific tasks, please contact me.

Previously, I was a PhD student in Computational Physics at Stanford and hold a BS with high honors from UC Berkeley. I co-authored an open standard for zero-knowledge verification of AI agent payments (ERC-8150), won 1st place at ETHDenver 2026 with VAEB, a guardrail for AI agent actions, shipped AI infrastructure serving 600k MAU, and have red-teamed 400+ systems in security reviews.

I am passionate about:

  • computational science + AI to expand human-knowledge boundaries faster
  • humanitarian science + AI for deeper comprehension of the past and sharper vision of the future
  • consensus-based guardrails for AI safety and alignment
