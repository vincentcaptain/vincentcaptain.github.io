Shujia Liang (Nathan)

Cofounder, Vishwa

I am the cofounder of Vishwa, an agent-native banking infrastructure that uses ZK proofs to guardrail agent transactions before they execute. My interests span AI, blockchain, computational physics, statistics, chemistry, history, and political economy.

Previously, I was a PhD student in Computational Physics at Stanford and hold a BS with high honors from UC Berkeley. I authored ERC-8150 (ZK Verification of Agent Transactions), won 1st place at ETH Denver Hackathon, shipped AI infrastructure serving 600k MAU, and have conducted 400+ protocol security reviews (zkSync, Mantle, Manta, etc.).

I am passionate about: AI + Fintech for autonomous capital flow with guardrails; computational science + AI to expand human-knowledge boundaries faster; and humanitarian science + AI for deeper comprehension of the past and sharper vision of the future.
